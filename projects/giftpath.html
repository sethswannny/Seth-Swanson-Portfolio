AI Product Case Study

GiftPath converts assessment answers into career recommendations.

GiftPath is an AI-powered career discovery platform that combines structured assessment data, scoring logic, job-market APIs, authentication, saved results, and subscription infrastructure into one product.

System Design

A recommendation product needs guardrails before it needs polish.

Scoring

Keep the foundation deterministic.

Assessment answers are converted into structured scores first. That gives the AI layer a stable input and makes the recommendation easier to explain, debug, and improve.

Recommendations

Use AI where language adds value.

The model is best used to translate scores into plain-language career reasoning, not to invent the entire result. That keeps the product useful while reducing opaque or inconsistent output.

01

Product database

Persist users, assessment responses, scores, saved results, subscriptions, and recommendation history so the product can support returning users.

02

External integrations

Connect payment, authentication, email, and job APIs so recommendations are tied to real product workflows instead of a static report.

03

AI output layer

Generate explainable career narratives from structured scores, then keep the output anchored to the assessment and visible match criteria.

What This Shows

I can ship AI products that still behave like systems.

GiftPath connects the pieces a real product needs: structured inputs, scoring rules, AI-assisted explanation, live external data, auth, payment flows, persistence, and an interface that makes the result feel usable.
