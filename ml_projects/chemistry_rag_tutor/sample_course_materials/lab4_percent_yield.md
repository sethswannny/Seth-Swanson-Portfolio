# Lab 4: Percent Yield

## Purpose

Students will compare theoretical yield to actual yield and explain sources of experimental error.

## Before Lab

- Complete the pre-lab safety check.
- Wear goggles throughout the experiment.
- Tie back loose hair and secure loose clothing.

## Report Requirements

- Balanced reaction table
- Measured mass data
- Limiting-reactant calculation
- Theoretical yield
- Percent yield
- Short error-analysis paragraph

## Due Date

The lab report is due at the next class meeting after the lab.
